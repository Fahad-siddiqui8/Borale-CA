The Noble Open Kettle Method

One does not become a Master Soapmaker overnight. At Borale, we revive the centuries-old boiled process in open kettles — the only method recognized internationally that yields true 72% Extra Pure soap.

01

Kettle Saponification

Pure plant oils are heated and slowly cooked inside traditional open kettles alongside electrolyzed sea salt alkali until full emulsion occurs.

Stage 1: Cooking
02

Salting Out & Purifying

Natural brine rinses are repeatedly washed through the soap paste to precipitate and remove any residual unreacted fats, impurities, and moisture.

Stage 2: Purification
03

Brine Washing & Fitting

The pure soap curd is settled to reach the coveted 72% minimum concentration of pure vegetable fatty acids, leaving it exceptionally gentle on human skin.

Stage 3: 72% Extra Pure
04

Cold Botanical Infusion

Essential oils are added only after cooling and thorough drying, ensuring the fragile aromatic and restorative terpenes are never degraded by heat.

Stage 4: Cold Blend

Naturally Raw: Less Than Six Ingredients

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Should Know

The 72% seal comes from the traditional Marseille soapmaking standard established in France in the 17th century. It denotes the minimum required fatty acid concentration in the finished soap curd after fully boiling and salting out all unreacted oils, water, and impurities. It certifies you are receiving authentic, dense, long-lasting pure soap without synthetic binders or water diluters.
Most commercial soaps heat their fragrances to over 100°C during manufacture, destroying the volatile therapeutic terpenes. Borale lets the open-kettle boiled soap paste thoroughly cool and dry before adding our 100% pure steam-distilled boreal essential oils, preserving their full aromatic spectrum and skin-soothing qualities.
Yes, absolutely. Our Extra Pure #01 Unscented bar and our Pure Shea Butter contain less than six 100% natural ingredients, with zero perfumes, essential oils, or artificial preservatives. They are hypoallergenic, pediatrician-recommended, and ideal for sensitive skin, babies, and eczema.
All our wild conifer needles (White Pine, Balsam Fir, White Spruce, and Cedar) are sustainably harvested and distilled directly from the Canadian boreal forest surrounding our workshop in Baie-Comeau, along the St. Lawrence North Shore in Quebec.
